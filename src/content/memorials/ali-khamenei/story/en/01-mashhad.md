Ali Khamenei was born in Mashhad on 19 April 1939. His father, Javad Khamenei, was a cleric born in Najaf; according to Wikipedia, his mother came from Yazd. He grew up in a city whose seminary passed religious learning from one generation to the next.

His first years of study took place in that Mashhad seminary. The path that began there would, a few years later, lead him to Qom.
