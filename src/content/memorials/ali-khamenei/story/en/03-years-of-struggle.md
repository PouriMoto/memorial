According to his official website, as cited on Wikipedia, SAVAK arrested him six times. The first arrest is placed after the 1963 demonstrations against Khomeini’s arrest.

He married in 1964 and, in 1976, was exiled for three years, until the end of the Shah’s rule. These years were marked by arrests and separation from his city, and they ended before the 1979 revolution.
