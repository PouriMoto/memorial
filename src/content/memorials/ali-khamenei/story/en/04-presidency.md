On 27 June 1981, at the Aboozar Mosque, a bomb hidden in a tape recorder exploded beside him. His arm, vocal cords and lungs were badly injured and he permanently lost the use of his right arm. He survived.

Months later, after the assassination of Mohammad-Ali Rajai, he was elected president in the October 1981 election and took office on 9 October. He was Iran’s third president and remained in office until 16 August 1989.
