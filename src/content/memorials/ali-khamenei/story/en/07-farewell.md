Iranian state media confirmed Ali Khamenei’s death on 28 February 2026, and the government declared 40 days of mourning. News reports say he was killed in an air strike during the 2026 war. He was 86.

Wikipedia reports that on 8 March 2026 the Assembly of Experts elected his son, Mojtaba Khamenei, as successor.
