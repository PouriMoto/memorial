After Khomeini’s death, the Assembly of Experts elected him on 4 June 1989. Wikipedia reports that he himself said he did not meet the marja‘ requirement the constitution then set, a remark that can be read as humility before a heavy responsibility.

A referendum on 28 July 1989 removed that requirement from the constitution, and on 6 August the Assembly confirmed him as permanent leader. He was the second Supreme Leader of the Islamic Republic.
