In 1970 she became a teacher at a girls' school and was given Classroom Number Three: a room with a window onto the courtyard and desks that still smelled of paint.

From the first week she began a habit that lasted to the end: every lesson started with a line of verse, and whoever could say what it meant could close their notebook early for break.
