Parvin was born in a narrow alley lined with orange trees in Shiraz. Her father was a carpenter and the house smelled of wood shavings; her mother told stories at night and never told the same one the same way twice.

Little Parvin learned early that words are like wood: you can plane them down and build something that holds a person's hand.
