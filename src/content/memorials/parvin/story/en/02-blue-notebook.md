At twelve she was given a blue-covered notebook and wrote her first poems in it. They were simple ones about the alley, her mother and the oranges. She showed the notebook to no one until, years later, she showed it to her students.

In high school she had a teacher who read poetry aloud. Right there she decided to do the same one day.
