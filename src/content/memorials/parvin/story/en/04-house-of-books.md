She married in 1974. Their new home had more bookshelves than sofas, and guests had to move books aside to sit down. Negar was born in 1977 and Kamran in 1981.

The children grew up on verse instead of lullabies. The night Kamran had a fever, Parvin recited ghazals until morning.
